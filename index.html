Good walks.
A bigger purpose.

Our commitment is part of how Packwalk is built.

Walker sharePlatform fee
80%

To your walker.

Walkers keep 80% of each walk fee. They set their own rates and availability.

20%

Our fee. Our commitment.

Packwalk commits its entire 20% platform fee to supporting Toronto animal rescues through company-funded giving.

You are buying a dog walk, not making a donation. This is Packwalk's commitment; no funds have been disbursed to rescues yet.

05 / The reason we're here

It started
with Astor.

He'd been adopted once and returned.
The reason? “Sheds too much.”

I'm Sandeep. Astor is my Great Pyrenees rescue, and the dog behind Packwalk. I built this app in Toronto because finding help with your dog should feel local and personal.

The rescue community gave dogs like Astor another chance. That's why supporting Toronto animal rescues is part of this company from the start.
